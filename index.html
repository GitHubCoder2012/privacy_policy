RollerControl 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日 · 运营者：广东东箭汽车科技股份有限公司

本政策说明 RollerControl 如何处理使用兼容电动卷帘设备时涉及的信息。应用无需注册或登录。当前版本通过低功耗蓝牙与附近的兼容设备通信，未接入广告、行为分析或开发者自有的云端账户服务。

1. 蓝牙和权限

应用使用蓝牙扫描发现附近的兼容设备，使用蓝牙连接发送控制指令并读取设备状态。Android 12 及以上版本请求“附近设备”的扫描和连接权限；Android 11 及以下版本因系统对蓝牙扫描的要求，请求前台定位权限。应用不使用蓝牙扫描结果推断或记录你的地理位置，也不请求后台定位权限。拒绝相关权限后，设备搜索和控制功能将无法正常使用。

2. 保存在本机的信息

应用在设备本机保存最近连接过的蓝牙设备标识与名称、语言选择，以及为恢复中断的固件操作所需的少量状态。应用还会在本机保存用于排查故障的运行日志，默认保留最近 3 天，并设置总容量上限。日志可能包含时间、错误、设备和通信相关的技术信息；应用会遮盖部分设备标识及 PIN/密码字段，但请在分享前自行检查内容。

3. 信息传输和分享

设备控制数据在手机与所连接的兼容蓝牙设备之间传输。当前版本不会自动将上述本机信息发送到开发者服务器。只有当你主动选择“导出日志”时，应用才会生成压缩文件并交给系统分享面板；是否发送、发送给谁由你选择。分享后，接收方会按其自身规则处理收到的文件。手机操作系统可能根据你的设备备份设置备份应用数据，该过程由系统控制。

4. 保存和删除

本机日志按上述保留期限自动清理。你可通过手机系统设置清除应用数据，或卸载应用，以删除手机上的应用数据；已经由你分享给其他接收方的文件不受此操作影响。系统备份副本的删除方式由相应备份服务决定。

5. 儿童与政策变更

本应用用于控制实体卷帘设备，并非面向儿童设计。如应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页及生效日期，并按适用要求通知用户。

6. 联系我们

有关隐私和数据处理的问题，请联系 广东东箭汽车科技股份有限公司：apple@dongjiancorp.com

RollerControl Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Operator: WINBO DONGJIAN AUTOMOTIVE TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD

This policy explains how RollerControl handles information when you use it with a compatible motorized roller shade. No account or sign-in is required. The current app communicates with nearby compatible devices over Bluetooth Low Energy and does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or a developer-operated cloud account service.

1. Bluetooth and permissions

The app scans for nearby compatible devices, connects to them, sends control commands, and reads device status. On Android 12 and later, it requests Nearby devices scan and connect permissions. On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires foreground Location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not use scan results to infer or record your geographic location and does not request background location. Denying these permissions prevents device discovery and control from working normally.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores identifiers and names of recently connected Bluetooth devices, your language choice, and a small amount of state needed to resume an interrupted firmware operation on your phone. It also stores diagnostic app logs locally. By default, logs are retained for up to three days and are subject to a total size limit. Logs may contain timestamps, errors, and technical device or communication details. The app masks some device identifiers and PIN/password fields, but you should review a log before sharing it.

3. Transfers and sharing

Control data passes between your phone and the connected compatible Bluetooth device. The current app does not automatically send the locally stored information described above to a developer server. Only when you select Export logs does the app create a ZIP file and hand it to the system share sheet. You choose whether to send it and to whom. The recipient handles a shared file under its own terms. Your phone's operating system may back up app data according to your device backup settings; that process is controlled by the operating system.

4. Retention and deletion

Local logs are automatically cleaned up according to the retention period above. You can clear the app's data in your phone settings or uninstall the app to remove its data from your phone. This does not remove files you have already shared with other recipients. Deletion of operating-system backup copies is governed by the relevant backup service.

5. Children and changes

This app controls physical shade hardware and is not designed for children. If its features or data handling change, we will update this page and its effective date and provide any notice required by applicable rules.

6. Contact

For questions about privacy or data handling, contact WINBO DONGJIAN AUTOMOTIVE TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD at apple@dongjiancorp.com
